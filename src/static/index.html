📚 Why 20 minutes of daylight works ▾
Hattar et al. · Science 2002

Melanopsin retinal cells (ipRGCs) synchronize the brain's master clock — screens can't trigger them, daylight can.

Lucas et al. · Trends in Neurosciences 2014

Equivalent Melanopic Lux (EML) peaks near 480 nm — the blue-cyan band abundant outdoors, absent behind glass.

Huberman Lab · Stanford

Morning daylight viewing anchors cortisol and sets a 14–16 h countdown to natural melatonin and deep sleep.
